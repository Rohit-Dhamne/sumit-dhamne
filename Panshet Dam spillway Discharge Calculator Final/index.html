⚠️ GOS Safety Guardrails (Panshet Dam)

  • Gates operate only in symmetric pairs -- never a single gate or three at once.
  • Opening order: Gate 1 & 4 (outer pair) first, then Gate 2 & 3 (inner pair) -- sequence 1‑4‑2‑3.
  • Closing order reverses: 3‑2‑4‑1.
  • No single continuous gate movement > 0.25 m (~0.82 ft); alternate outer/inner pairs in steps of ≤0.5 m until target opening or full clearance is reached.
